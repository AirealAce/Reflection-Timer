    Display and behavior

    TextInput

    Display

    These options save immediately. Prompts stay out of Alt+Tab. Only one reflection is visible at a time; Prev and Next save your draft locally and browse pending reflections.

    Reflection prompts

    After Save, reopen a response to continue after this separator. Applies to session-end and check-in reflections. Empty responses stay empty. Saves immediately.

    On by default. When another session ends, older unfinished session reflections (including blank or closed drafts) are queued and marked “auto-sent”. The new reflection and active check-ins remain available for writing. Turn this off to keep older drafts for Prev/Next review. Practice prompts never auto-send real reflections. Offline entries remain in Outbox.

    Dragging saves a custom position, including across app and PC restarts. Presets use that screen; if it is disconnected, the timer stays on an available screen. Duration fields are shared with the main timer and editable while stopped or paused.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    Ctrl+Tab switches to the next App tab. Ctrl+Shift+Tab switches to the previous tab. Both wrap around and work from inputs; an open dialog keeps focus.

    Space or Ctrl+Enter starts, resumes, or pauses the timer anywhere in Compact or Time-only view, like Enter in a duration field. In Settings, Ctrl+Enter or Ctrl+S saves all pending settings from any focused control or page text. In a reflection prompt, Ctrl+Enter or Alt+S sends the response and ends its own session early if it is still running or paused. Both skip when both fields are completely empty. Ctrl+S saves the draft locally and closes. Alt+Enter sends a check-in without ending the timer.

    Ctrl+R resets the timer to the duration inputs and refreshes the focused view, including session-end prompts. The timer becomes ready to start. Your App tab, Compact or Time-only mode, and reflection drafts are retained. Save unfinished settings before refreshing.

    Saves immediately for regular, scheduled, and test reflections. Uses the screen containing your mouse pointer when the popup opens, leaving space for the taskbar. An already-open reflection stays where it is.

    Audio

    Disruptive stops other app audio. Assertive lowers other app audio to 25% until it finishes. Polite plays alongside other app audio. The latest Assertive sound takes priority; earlier sounds recover when it finishes. Other apps are unaffected.

    seconds remaining

    Use threshold starts checked at 15 seconds. Uncheck it to turn off low-on-time audio. These controls stay in sync with Timer and save automatically; scheduled sessions keep their own choices. Save settings, Ctrl+Enter, or Ctrl+S also saves pending edits.

    Low-on-time audio plays once per session. If a session starts within its threshold, it alerts on its first tick. Ending near zero counts as a normal completion within the threshold, or 15 seconds when Use threshold is unchecked, capped at 10% of the session duration. Time spent is still recorded accurately.

    App sound controls all app audio and matches the Timer slider. Each audio volume below scales this level.

    Sound selections, volume levels, playback modes, and fade-out settings autosave silently. Volume changes apply to playing audio too. Fade out after applies to each new playback; unchecked sounds play to their normal end. Previews with Fade out after enabled play through the selected delay and one-second fade, unless the track ends sooner. Other previews stop after 5 seconds. This applies to Preview audio and previews when selecting a sound. None is silent. Each new preview replaces the previous one; Stop all app audio stops it immediately. All audio uses App sound × its audio volume. A scheduled session adopts its saved App sound level when it starts. Custom files stay at their selected location; audio paths are never uploaded.

    Startup & diagnostics

    Reflection Timer starts in the tray using your saved settings.

    Save settings

    Ctrl+Enter or the bottom-right Save settings button saves your settings from anywhere on this page.

    The API token, reflections, and drafts are encrypted for your Windows account in %USERPROFILE%\.reflection-timer. This is the single active settings folder for every launch method. Connection settings may be saved incomplete; sending waits until they are valid. Windows sign-in startup is optional and off by default.

    Google Sheets connection

    Your existing desktop connection is retained when upgrading. You can also use the timer and save reflections before connecting to Google Sheets.

    Set up a new Google Sheets receiver
    1. Create or choose your own Google spreadsheet. Enter its URL below.
    2. Choose Save private receiver script. A new token is generated if the token field is blank and none is saved.
    3. In your spreadsheet, open Extensions, then Apps Script. Paste the saved script into the editor and save it.
    4. Run setupReflectionTimer once and authorize it with the Google account that owns the sheet.
    5. Deploy a new web app that executes as you, with access set to Anyone. Copy its deployed URL ending in /exec into the Receiver URL field.
    6. Stop the other timer or browser extension from sending to this sheet, select Enable Sheets delivery, then choose Save & test connection. The app verifies the receiver before enabling delivery.

    The token protects requests to the receiver. Keep your personalized script and setup code private.

    Leave blank to keep the saved token. No saved token yet.

    Transfer a private connection setup code

    A setup code contains your sheet connection and token. It is not encrypted. Keep it private.

    Review guide
    
      
    1. Enter a duration, leave the input, and read the surrounding text at your own pace.
    2. Start the timer. Read this page or another app for a minute. The countdown should not interrupt you.
    3. Use Read remaining time. You should hear the current time once.
    4. Open the compact timer and try both views using the keyboard.
    5. Navigate individual Scheduler and Outbox cells using your reader's table commands.
    6. Write a practice reflection, choose Save, and reopen it to check that the draft is preserved.
    
      
    Settings, audio, connection setup, delivery, and diagnostics use your saved desktop profile. Practice reflections are sent to the test tab when delivery is enabled.
    
      
    App, the floating timer, and session-end prompts are separate windows. Compact and Time-only share one floating window. App opens centered, the floating timer bottom left, and session-end prompts bottom right. Closing App hides it in the tray; choose Quit desktop app to exit.
    
      
    Ctrl+Space or Ctrl+Alt+Space starts, resumes, or pauses the timer globally, including while its windows are hidden. The Ctrl+Alt shortcuts are available when another running app has not already registered them: T for App, , to shrink or hide the floating timer, . to focus Compact (twice for App), / for a check-in, ' (apostrophe) to switch Timer / Stopwatch, and ` to end early. You can also open views from the tray icon.
