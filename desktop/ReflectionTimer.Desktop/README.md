# Reflection Timer 4.2.47

Long explanations are collapsed by default. Use the circled question mark beside a section heading to open or close its guidance with a click, Enter, or Space. The Help tab collects instructions and keyboard shortcuts; Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab include Help. Settings → Help and guidance → Show all explanations keeps the explanations open by default and saves immediately. Labels, current state, delivery issues, and errors stay visible. Compact, Time-only, and reflection-popup layouts are unchanged.

Stopwatch: use Ctrl+Alt+' (apostrophe) from any app, S/T beside the Compact or Time-only minus button, or Switch to Stopwatch/Timer in App view. Switching pauses and preserves the other session; resume explicitly. The regular start/pause/reset controls and global start/pause shortcuts act on the selected mode. Stopwatch has no duration inputs or auto-start. Ctrl+Alt+/ pauses it, plays session-end audio, and opens its reflection. Save / Ctrl+S resumes the same selected stopwatch and keeps the response; Save & send finishes it with active elapsed time only. A parked draft never resumes a second clock. Time reached audio sits below Low on time in Settings and defaults to Battle (Champion), with one alert at 300 active seconds. Existing profiles without a separate stopwatch sound keep inheriting their low-time sound until edited. Scheduled sessions remain countdowns.

Apps Script receiver 2.9.2+ writes C active duration, D allotted duration (blank for stopwatches), E status, F the reason for ending early, and G exactly `timer` or `stop watch`. This applies to new entries; historical rows are unchanged. Stopwatch delivery requires receiver 2.9.0+, but update to 2.9.2+ for the new column layout. Receivers without stopwatch support leave these entries safely in Outbox for retry after updating the existing deployment. Generate the receiver from Connection setup using your existing sheet/token and deployment, never a public copy of your credentials. Timer entries remain compatible with existing receivers.

App view places the running/end-time status directly below the desktop-active notice. Beneath its clock, Compact-style controls provide Auto-start, Comp (hide the floating viewer, or show full Compact controls when hidden), Reset, Start/Pause/Resume, and End early; the original text controls remain available.

“Use threshold” starts unchecked with 13 seconds retained on a new installation. Checking it enables low-on-time audio and makes the number editable; unchecking it disables the warning and retains the number for re-enabling. Timer and Settings → Audio → Low on time audio share this preference and autosave changes. Save settings / Ctrl+Enter / Ctrl+S also flushes pending threshold edits. Scheduler has the same control but saves each appointment's own enabled state, threshold, and sound, retaining its inherited 15-second threshold. Timer/Settings changes do not change existing scheduled choices. Existing saved enabled states, thresholds, and audio selections are retained on upgrade.

Ending within a grace period counts as a normal completion: use the ending session's effective low-time threshold when Use threshold is checked, otherwise 15 seconds. Cap that period at 10% of the allotted duration. The exact boundary is included, and paused time does not advance it. This classification applies to all completion paths, preserves actual elapsed time, and remains attached to the completed reflection when settings or the next session change. A normal completion omits the early-end reason from delivery.

The accessible interface is now the primary desktop app. Normal launches use the encrypted `%USERPROFILE%\.reflection-timer` profile, independent of a launcher's AppData virtualization. First launch copies and verifies the old ReflectionTimerDesktop profile, retaining its Sheets connection, settings, drafts, schedules and Outbox and leaving the original untouched. A pre-existing shared profile is never replaced by a legacy copy. No personal connection settings belong in the application package.

The four views, themes, audio controls, window positions and Ctrl+Alt shortcuts are retained. App and reflection prompts can coexist with either Compact or Time-only. Semantic HTML supports screen-reader web navigation; quiet countdown updates do not interrupt reading.

On cold startup, App and Compact keep their web controls hidden behind the saved theme's native background until preferences are restored. App also waits for its Settings fields to load, so an early Save cannot replace preferences with HTML defaults. If loading fails, the app shows an error instead of pretending default settings are your saved choices. Optional local diagnostics record startup and the loaded theme (0 Dark, 1 Light, 2 High Contrast, 3 Glamour); no setting credentials or reflection text are logged.

Reflection prompts show the session date and time right-aligned beside the local draft status, below the writing area. The timestamp follows the selected reflection when using Prev/Next and updates when a check-in becomes a completed session.

Ctrl+Space or Ctrl+Alt+Space starts, resumes, or pauses the timer globally while the app is running, including when another app has focus or all timer windows are hidden. It uses the shared duration inputs and keeps focus in the current app. Time-only stays small when starting, pausing, or resuming. Its bottom-left transport button appears on hover or keyboard focus, matches the Compact transport colors, and keeps the same small window when activated. Holding the keys toggles only once. Space or Ctrl+Enter starts, resumes, or pauses the timer anywhere in the focused Compact or Time-only viewer, just like Enter in a duration field. It preserves validation and pause/resume behavior without activating other focused buttons, inserting spaces in duration inputs, or scrolling the page. Holding a key does not repeatedly toggle the timer. Open dialogs retain their own keys; Space in App or a reflection keeps its normal behavior.

In a reflection window, Ctrl+Enter or Alt+S sends the response and ends its own session early if it is still running or paused. The native timer records actual elapsed time, respects auto-start and its cutoff, and cannot stop a newer timer when sending an older reflection. A send at or after the deadline records normal completion. Both shortcuts skip if both fields are completely empty, with success audio. Sending requires response text; partial drafts remain editable if validation fails. Ctrl+S saves locally and closes, even for an empty or reason-only draft. Alt+Enter and Save & send retain their check-in behavior without ending the timer. Escape saves and closes, or skips if both fields are empty; empty Alt+Enter also skips. Save and Ctrl+Alt+slash continue to save drafts locally. Saved responses and reasons survive natural completion, and normal delivery success/failure feedback remains enabled.

Settings → Audio → Low on time audio includes “Fade out after message sent” and an editable duration in whole seconds (five seconds initially, checkbox on by default for both Low on time and Time reached). Send starts fading that session's currently playing alert immediately, before response validation, draft storage, or Sheets delivery. This applies to Save & send, Ctrl+Enter, Alt+S, and Alt+Enter. A validation, storage, or delivery failure keeps the response available but does not undo the fade; retries cannot restart or lengthen it. Save, Skip (including empty send shortcuts), practice entries, automatic/background delivery, and manually marking an entry sent do not trigger it. Sending an older reflection cannot fade a newer session's warning. The existing playback-duration fade and natural track ending may finish sooner. Disruptive audio still stops other sounds immediately; use Polite or Assertive for overlapping audio when you want to hear the fade. Changes autosave silently and are included in Save settings / Ctrl+Enter / Ctrl+S.

Settings → Reflection prompts includes “Continue saved reflections with”: None, Comma, Bullet (newline and `- `), or Newline (default). Save, including the reflection shortcut, prepares the separator for the next visit and places the caret after it. Empty responses remain empty; reopening without typing does not accumulate separators. Unused separators are omitted from submitted responses. The setting also applies to check-ins.

Prev/Next saves both fields and loads the selected reflection in the same window and WebView2 control. Navigation does not recreate browser controls or submit reflections. A failed load restores the previous saved editor. Recoverable GPU/utility failures or temporary renderer stalls no longer hide a working interface; local diagnostics record WebView2 failure kind, reason, and exit code without page text.

Closing Compact/Time-only or a reflection now hides and reuses its browser control. Closed reflections are excluded from editing, shortcuts, and draft flushing until reopened; a new prompt resets the editor before display. This avoids tearing down WebView2 accessibility objects during normal viewer switching.

Shrinking or hiding the floating timer returns keyboard focus to the last usable window outside Reflection Timer. Moving through App, Compact, or reflection views does not replace that return destination with another timer window. A background view change leaves another application's focus alone; owned modal dialogs keep their focus.

Settings → Display → Hide viewer [3] seconds after running can hide Compact/Time-only automatically for either Timer or Stopwatch. The number is editable; it is disabled by default, with 3 seconds retained. Starting or resuming the session begins the delay, including scheduled starts, automatic restarts, and resuming a stopwatch after saving its reflection. Opening, focusing, resizing, switching floating layouts, or reopening the app does not begin or restart the delay. While this option is enabled, pausing or resetting cancels pending hiding and shows the saved floating layout again without taking keyboard focus, including after a hidden/tray-only launch. Reset also restores the viewer when the selected session is already ready or at zero; cancelled or failed resets leave it unchanged. Turning the option off cancels pending hiding and retains ordinary manual visibility behavior. Editing a pending delay retains its original session start/resume timestamp. Sessions continue after hiding, and App/reflection windows are unaffected. Reopen with the usual view shortcuts or tray menu. Native modal dialogs and dragging postpone hiding; no background countdown runs while disabled or hidden.

View choices are saved when you change them. Closing App view with X or its toggle shortcut remembers that it is hidden; shrinking/expanding the floating timer remembers Compact versus Time-only. Quitting and reopening the app restores those choices and the saved floating position, without putting a hidden App view back in Alt+Tab. Explicitly opening App view from the tray, its shortcut, or the floating viewer still works. `--tray` forces App view hidden for that launch without overwriting its saved preference. Shutdown/sign-out queries do not change these preferences or veto Windows shutdown.

Ctrl+Alt+comma cycles Compact → Time-only → hidden → Compact. Ctrl+Alt+Shift+comma reverses that order: Compact → hidden → Time-only → Compact. Entering Time-only releases focus to the previous usable window (including shrinking with minus/Escape or automatically on start); if another window already has focus, it keeps it. Showing a hidden Time-only viewer does not activate it. Controls remain clickable and Compact expansion still focuses the timer controls. Rapid shortcut presses use the latest requested layout, ignoring stale browser size reports. A closed/minimized/disabled prior window is not focused, and owned modal dialogs are not bypassed.

Settings → Audio → Voice announcements optionally speaks Timer/Stopwatch started, paused, resumed, and reset. Timer speaks remaining time; Stopwatch speaks elapsed time. Reset speaks the resulting duration, for example “Timer reset to 20 minutes” or “Stopwatch reset.” The time portion is omitted at 0:00. Reset feedback follows a successful saved reset only, not a canceled confirmation or failed save. It also covers global shortcuts, scheduled/repeating timer starts, mode-switch pauses, and a stopwatch resumed by saving its reflection. It does not add completion speech to the existing session-end audio or announce a fake start when loading saved state. App voice is off by default for new profiles and profiles without this setting. Explicit saved choices, including voice enabled, are retained on upgrades. The checkbox autosaves, and Preview voice also works while it is off.

Screen-reader feedback is independent of optional app speech and App sound volume: identical action/time, reset, mode, and view messages are raised through a single stable Windows UI Automation notification provider, including when viewers are hidden or unfocused. No focus change or extra popup is needed. Visible documents also retain non-live readable status; if the native notification API fails, the existing live-region path is used. Redundant terse command replies are suppressed, and neither failed saves nor periodic clock updates announce false changes. View notifications request CurrentThenMostRecent processing so they do not interrupt an ongoing notification. JAWS and other screen readers use their own voices, rates, and notification preferences; enabling both channels may produce both voices. See [Microsoft's notification API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/system.windows.forms.accessibleobject.raiseautomationnotification?view=windowsdesktop-10.0) and [notification processing behavior](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/uiautomationcore/ne-uiautomationcore-notificationprocessing). Notification API tests are not proof of actual JAWS speech; that requires a separate screen-reader test.

The shared voice preset is **Microsoft Hazel Desktop, speed +3, pitch +20%**, matching the hotkey-announcement voice. Every installation prefers this preset without needing the hotkey app or personal configuration files. If Hazel is missing, it prefers another British English feminine voice, then US English feminine, then another feminine voice, then Windows' default. The exact voice therefore depends on installed Windows voices; no voice package is bundled or copied from another PC. To obtain Hazel, use **Windows Settings → Time & language → Language & region → English (United Kingdom) → Language options**, and install its **Text-to-speech** feature (add the language first if necessary). You do not need to change the Windows display language. Restart Reflection Timer afterward, then use **Preview voice**. See [Microsoft's text-to-speech installation instructions](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/education/learning-accelerators/download-languages-and-voices-for-immersive-reader-read-mode-and-read-aloud). Windows versions and voice packages may differ; speech falls back instead of blocking the timer.

The same voice option also announces Timer/Stopwatch mode switches and Compact, Time-only, floating-hidden, and App visibility changes. A switch that pauses an outgoing session combines its pause/time and the destination mode in one utterance. View feedback waits for an action/time announcement to finish; only the latest waiting view is retained. Automatic shrink therefore cannot cut off “Timer started with … remaining.” Startup/restoration and selecting an unchanged mode stay quiet.

Focus on/off now uses that same pair of feedback channels, whether changed through Ctrl+Alt+;, Timer, or Settings. With no target or idle trigger selected, the shortcut opens the chooser and gives guidance instead of claiming Focus is enabled. Shortcut failures, unavailable reflections and global reset failures also reach optional app speech and independent native screen-reader notifications, including while hidden. Changing Focus delays/targets, repeated saves, background status polling and routine autosaves do not trigger app speech. Failed saves never announce a successful toggle.

Speech uses Windows SAPI locally, follows App sound volume, and plays alongside MP3 audio. New action speech replaces older speech. Disabling the option, muting App sound, Stop all app audio, and quitting cancel both current speech and queued view feedback. Speech initialization/playback run on a separate background thread; a missing voice cannot stop or undo the timer. Pitch markup contains only escaped app-generated text. No microphone, network speech service, reflection text, or new dependency is used, and Windows' system-wide voice preferences are not changed.

If a renderer exits, its document reloads. If the shared browser exits, all viewers rebuild together after WebView2 releases the old process. Recovery reloads saved drafts/settings and preserves timer deadlines, window visibility, and the floating view's mode. It never submits a reflection. Text not yet saved before a crash cannot be recovered. Repeated crashes stop automatic retries and offer a keyboard-accessible Retry interface button. Diagnostics record recovery outcomes without page text.

Settings provides separate always-on-top switches for Compact, Time-only, and reflection prompts, all enabled by default. Prompts are excluded from Alt+Tab. Only one reflection is visible at a time. Bottom-left Prev and Next save both draft fields locally before opening the adjacent pending reflection; browsing never submits it. At either end the unavailable button is disabled. Save and Save & send still close the current window without automatically opening another.

Auto-send incomplete reflections when a session ends is checked by default and saves immediately. A new completion queues older unfinished session reflections (including blank or closed drafts), preserving early-end status and reason and marking them auto-sent. Turn it off to retain older drafts for navigation. The new completion, active check-ins, and the other test/real mode are excluded. Opening a saved or practice prompt never triggers auto-send. An unsent check-in is promoted into its own completion under the same ID, preserving saved text and in-flight edits. Already-submitted entries are never copied or resent. Auto-send first commits to Outbox; failed local saves leave the previous draft editable and the new prompt pending. Network delivery uses the existing connection and retry rules.

Settings → Reflection prompts → Show session-end popups is on by default. Turning it off suppresses automatic completion windows for regular, repeating, and scheduled timers without discarding the new reflection. Sounds, auto-start, and the separate auto-send-incomplete-reflections policy are unchanged. Pending reflections, reflection shortcuts, explicit end-early/stopwatch review, test prompts, and Prev/Next remain available. An already-open editor is not closed by changing this setting. It saves immediately and survives restarts; re-enabling affects future completions without opening old prompts in bulk.

The reason field is available while a check-in's session is still running/paused and for a genuine early ending. It disappears after natural completion, independently of any next session. Provisional reasons stay with the draft and are submitted only for an actual early ending. New reflection windows load their saved text and theme before becoming visible.

If a target editor fails to load, navigation retains the current editor and draft. The failed hidden window is disposed and removed so retry can create a fresh one. An unavailable current editor cannot auto-send an older saved snapshot as if its latest text had been flushed; recovery must finish before it can be replaced.

At natural completion, early ending, and scheduled handoff, the session-end audio's behavior controls overlapping low-time audio: Polite mixes normally, Assertive reduces the other audio to 25% and restores it afterward, and Disruptive stops it. Explicit pause/reset/new-start, disabling low-time audio, or Stop all app audio can still stop playback.

New installations default to the Original extension sound (Assertive), Level Up for success, Out of Health for failure, Battle (Trainer) for low time, and Battle (Champion) for the stopwatch alert; all but session-end audio are Disruptive. App sound starts at 50%, each sound at 100%. Both low-time and stopwatch alerts enable timed fading (after 25 and 6 seconds respectively) and message-sent fading (over 5 seconds). Session-end, success, and failure fades start disabled, retaining 10 seconds for timed fading and 3 seconds for message-sent fading. Upgrades retain all saved choices, including legacy profiles without per-event audio settings. No personal audio paths or connection data are used as defaults.

Compact has a two-line Auto/Start toggle that lights up when enabled. Screen readers announce it as the "Auto-start next session" toggle button; Enter switches it without starting the timer. The narrower editor retains all three duration fields and the App, reset, start/pause, and end-session actions.

The App button stays highlighted while App view is shown, even when another window has focus. It dims when App view is hidden or minimized. Its accessible description reports that state; clicking it always shows App view or brings it forward.

After completion, App, Compact, and Time-only derive the displayed time directly from the duration boxes. Zero-valued finished updates and late running frames cannot overwrite this preview. Read-time feedback follows the same inputs; all-zero inputs keep 0:00. Paused sessions retain their remaining time until the duration is edited, and active/repeating/scheduled sessions keep counting down. Completed-session accounting is unchanged.

Open START-HERE.html for setup and keyboard help. Normal launches use the installed profile. Developers may use --profile NAME for isolated test data; old preview samples remain local. APP-PAGE-AUDIT.md records the earlier interface parity review.

Reflection prompts use Save to keep a local draft and close; Save & send remains the submission action. Ctrl+Alt+slash focuses the first reflection box when neither box is focused, and acts as Save when either box is focused. From another window it brings a reflection forward without saving, reopens the latest pending draft, or opens a running/paused-session check-in. It never redirects to App. Failed draft saves leave the text editable and the prompt open.
